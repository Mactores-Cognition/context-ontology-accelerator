Why raising concurrency buys less than it looks

Only three waves hold more than one stack, and the middle of the graph is a hard serial chain: storage → vkg → namespace → serve → sources. Both long poles sit inside it — storage (Neptune + OpenSearch Serverless) and serve (AgentCore Runtime). So the critical path is 10 waves deep whatever the concurrency, and parallelism only compresses the 5 independent foundation stacks at L0 and the two pairs at L6/L7.

Caveat on the time estimates. The only published per-stack durations are the ~Deploy Time column in infra/README.md, which is stale — it lists a coa-dev-structured stack that no longer exists and omits five that do. This view deliberately counts waves rather than minutes; where a figure is shown it is labelled indicative.
